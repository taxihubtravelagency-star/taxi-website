Good to know

Frequently asked
questions.

Need help with a specific route or itinerary? Contact us directly and we’ll discuss your requirements.

How do I request a taxi quote?+

Use the quick enquiry form or contact us by phone or WhatsApp. Include pickup, destination, travel date, passengers, vehicle preference and trip type for a useful quote.

Can I enquire about one-way taxi service?+

Yes. Share your route and date, and ask us to confirm the available vehicle options and current one-way fare.

Are the displayed tour packages fixed itineraries?+

The package cards are trip themes to start a conversation. Contact us to discuss route sequence, travel dates, vehicle requirements and current details.

Do you publish taxi fares online?+

Contact us for current fares. Rates depend on trip requirements and must be confirmed before booking.

How can I read customer reviews?+

Use the View All Reviews button to visit the Uttarakhand Taxi Service Google Business listing and see current customer feedback.

Do you provide cab service in Haldwani and Kathgodam?+

Yes. Tell us your pickup point in Haldwani or Kathgodam, your destination, date and vehicle preference, and we will share the available options and fare by phone or WhatsApp.

Can I book an airport taxi service?+

Yes. Enquire for airport and station pickups or drops. Share your flight or train time, pickup point and passenger count to get a quote.

Do you offer Haldwani to Delhi and Delhi to Haldwani taxi service?+

Yes, you can enquire for one-way or return trips in both directions. Share your date, passengers and vehicle preference for the current fare.
